How AURA Works

AURA is not a simple related-products widget. It is an agentic system that continuously observes your behavior, reasons over it, and generates personalized course recommendations grounded in the real catalog.

The Agent Loop

01

Observe

Page views, searches, clicks, and time spent are tracked in a non-blocking way and stored as behavioral events.

02

Understand

The LangGraph agent infers interests, skill level, and intent, then builds a living profile that updates as you explore.

03

Retrieve

Semantic search over the vector catalog returns grounded candidates — not invented courses.

Why it feels personal

Triggers

Smart when to call AI

High-intent actions, search bursts, staleness, and cooldowns avoid wasteful LLM calls on every click.

Dual-write

Catalog stays searchable

Every product is written to SQL and the vector store together so recommendations stay in sync.

Persuasion

Narrative, not a bare list

A short AIDA-style story explains why these courses fit your journey, then links the products.
